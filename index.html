Your board over time

From the Founder Board up, you start with five. It keeps pace with you.

Four times a year a new expert joins, added to your bench or swapped for one you have outgrown. Each is chosen for where you are then, and your monthly fee stays the same.

  1. Month 1

    Your five arrive

    After the intake, SuperStories picks five advisors for your situation. They know your market, your numbers, and what you are trying to do.

  2. Every quarter

    A new mind, for where you are now

    Four moves across the year. Hiring your first salesperson? A sales operator joins for it. Outgrown a seat? Trade it for the mind you need next.

  3. Always yours to shape

    You decide who sits

    Keep your five, swap a seat, or grow your active board to eight. The roster is yours to shape.

  4. Any time

    Call in a board for one decision

    A pricing call, a key hire, a launch that has to land. Put together a temporary board just for that question, then go back to your regular five.

Five to start. Four moves a year to keep it sharp. Same price throughout.

About

Why Growth Intel exists

Entrepreneurs make their most critical decisions alone. The right thinking partner is rarely available at the moment it matters.

The name is literal. Intel is the insight you normally never get, because the people who hold it are expensive, booked, or somewhere else. Growth is what it is for.

Growth Intel is built by SuperStories. The intake, the curation, the calibration: that's human work, done by SuperStories' strategy lead and founder. The technology is simple. The knowledge isn't.

Inspiration: Growth Intel is inspired by Abi Awomosu's Billion Dollar Focus Group methodology: the principle that the right questions, asked by the right people, deliver more value than a million data points.
